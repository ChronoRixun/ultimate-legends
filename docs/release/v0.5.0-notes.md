Ultimate Legends 0.5.0 — character models for X-Men Legends, Marvel: Ultimate Alliance (2006 PC) support, and
your own artwork and game order. Everything from the eight 0.5.0 betas is in it.

Portable build: unzip anywhere and run `ultimate-legends.exe`, or let 0.4.0 update itself. No game files are
included; every game runs from your own copy. SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Full list of changes: `CHANGELOG.md`.

### Character models (X-Men Legends)
- A **Character models** section on the X-Men Legends page: **Off** (the original models), **Community pack**
  (community-made skins, credited to their authors; needs nothing else), **Upgraded** (per hero the best of Marvel:
  Ultimate Alliance 2, Marvel: Ultimate Alliance and the community pack; needs one of those games set up in the
  launcher) and, under More, **Sharpened classic** and **XML2 look**.
- Per hero you see where the model comes from and why, a **costume chooser** where a source has several (named
  costumes and who made them, with a preview once a community skin is downloaded), and its status.
- **Apply** converts the models on your PC and puts them in the game's `mods` folder; your other mods stay. Restart
  the game to see them. **Remove** / **Off** takes them out again. When X-Men Legends was rebuilt since the models
  were made, the section offers **Rebuild**.
- **The launcher never ships a model.** The legends-models tool reads your own installs; community skins come from
  their author's own links and every file is checked. Before the first download the section shows BaconWizard17's
  terms (don't share the files outside marvelmods.com, don't sell them, credit him); nothing is downloaded until you
  agree.
- The tool (legends-models 0.3.0) installs and updates from the section, like the builder, on the Stable and the
  Beta update channel. With `-offline` nothing is downloaded, and the section says which heroes fall back to another
  source or the original model.
- The **Downloads** tab shows the models' work while it runs (downloading a skin, converting hero 3 of 15,
  installing) with **Cancel**, and keeps finished work, patch updates included, under **Recent**.

### Marvel: Ultimate Alliance (2006 PC)
- **Set up from your own disc.** Choose **Set up**, pick your retail PC DVD ISO or extracted DVD folder and a new or
  empty game folder. The launcher downloads the checked MUA Classic Fix setup package and the original dgVoodoo2
  archive (by Dege), then imports and verifies your game files on your PC without running the original installer.
  The package includes a source-built SafeDiscLoader2 (by nckstwrt). Game.exe stays unchanged, and no game files
  are downloaded. A cancelled or failed setup is never registered and cleans up after itself, even on slow drives.
- **An options page**: display mode, resolution, frame rate limit, VSync, Xbox controller support, mouse capture,
  Run in background, OpenSpy online and the graphics watermark. Each setting is written on its own and keeps the
  rest of the game's configuration; they can't be changed while the game runs.
- **Your MUA Classic Fix version, and Update in place.** The page shows the installed version (with a Beta badge
  for a test version) and offers **Update** when your update channel has a newer one. Update refreshes only the
  compatibility files: no disc needed, and your Game.exe, saves, mods and settings are kept (new settings are added
  without replacing your choices). Close the game first. A failed update rolls back, and if the launcher is closed
  in the middle, the next **Update** finishes or rolls back from its backups before you play. A locked file asks
  you to close the program using it and try again. Update also works through a game folder that is a junction or
  symbolic link on Windows.

### Marvel: Ultimate Alliance (2006 PC) on Steam Deck / Linux (Proton)
- Setup, Update and Play work under Proton, with MUA Classic Fix 0.2.2 (the launcher fetches it). Put the game
  folder on a Linux filesystem (the Deck's internal storage, an ext4 SD card or your home folder), not an NTFS or
  network drive.
- Under Proton the game uses Proton's own graphics instead of dgVoodoo2, and Proton handles the display mode, so
  the page hides the dgVoodoo2 display options there. Controller, Run in background, mouse and online options stay.
- **Moving a Windows-made MUA 2006 folder to the Deck? Choose Update.** The page explains why: Update keeps a backup
  of the Windows graphics file (`D3D9.dll`, under `_muac/backups`), removes it so Proton's graphics are used, and
  keeps your game files, saves, mods and settings. On Windows, nothing changes.
- Tested under GE-Proton 11 on Linux up to the game's main menu; see the known issues.

### Your artwork and your game order
- **Choose your own game artwork.** Right-click a game's card (or open its menu with the keyboard or a controller)
  and pick **Change artwork...** or **Change banner...**. Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP picture of up to 20 MB from your
  PC and check the preview; the launcher keeps its own copy. **Reset artwork** brings the defaults back. Nothing is
  fetched online, and your pictures are kept across restarts, under Proton too.
- Card titles, year tags and actions stay readable over your pictures, even very bright ones; custom banners keep
  their text readable too. The default artwork looks as before.
- **Rearrange your games.** Drag cards in the Library or icons in the sidebar, or use **Move earlier** / **Move
  later** from a card's menu. **Settings > Maintenance > Reset game order** brings the default order back and keeps
  your artwork.
- **New X-Men Legends card art** in the same sunrise style as the other games.

### Controller and keyboard
- **Every drop-down is drawn inside the launcher** (language, theme, update channel, the X-Men Legends and X-Men
  Legends II Display rows, the costume choosers) and opens with the mouse, touch, the keyboard or a controller:
  Enter / A opens it, Up / Down move, Enter / A picks, Escape / B closes it without a change. Under Proton (Steam
  Deck, Linux) drop-downs used to show nothing when clicked; they open now. On Windows they look and work as before.
- Every MUA 2006 option and the card menu (artwork, Move earlier / Move later) work with the keyboard or a
  controller as well as the mouse.
- In the beta confirmation, **Switch back to Stable** is the highlighted choice, and the update-channel box keeps
  the focus while it checks the new channel.

### Classic conversation box (X-Men Legends)
- **Classic conversation box (XML1 look)** is an optional appearance choice. It starts off. With Legends Classic
  builder 0.6.0-beta.3 or newer installed, turn it on and rebuild to use the blue background from your own XML1
  source; turn it off and rebuild to restore the PC look. With an older builder the option stays hidden; a newer
  builder that is only available, not installed, does not show it. No Legends Classic 0.6.0-beta.3 builder is out
  yet, so for now the option is hidden for everyone; it appears once that builder is installed (the Beta update
  channel gets it first).

### Other changes and fixes
- X-Men Legends setup also accepts an extracted XML1 install folder. You still need your own X-Men Legends II for PC.
- **First X-Men Legends builds work in a new folder on the Beta channel.** The launcher installs the XML2 Fix from
  your update channel before the builder starts and checks it suits that builder; Resume and Rebuild do the same.
  A failed or cancelled download leaves no partial fix behind.
- **Changing the update channel takes effect without a restart.** The X-Men Legends page, the setup wizard and the
  Character models section show what the new channel offers straight away, and every build checks the builder
  again, so switching to Beta no longer builds with an old Stable builder first.
- Cancelling Rebuild before it changes a finished build keeps the game ready. An interrupted build still offers
  Resume.
- **Save import is not in 0.5.0.** The X-Men Legends save-import preview that the 0.5.0 betas showed is hidden in
  this release; save import is planned for a later one.
- Building into another folder keeps the first folder's XML2 Fix record. An XML2 Fix that is too old now has an
  **Install the XML2 Fix** button after a refused Rebuild, and the wizard says a builder update is installed when
  you build.

### Known limitations
- Converting character models takes a while: about half a minute per hero from Marvel: Ultimate Alliance, several
  minutes for some Marvel: Ultimate Alliance 2 heroes. The game must be closed while the models change.
- Some costume names are still shown by number ("MUA1 costume 0801").
- MUA 2006 under Proton was tested under GE-Proton 11 on Linux, not yet on a real Steam Deck: setup, Update of a
  Windows-made folder and starting the game to its main menu work; gameplay past the menu, controllers through Steam
  Input, saves and online play are not confirmed yet. Please tell us how it goes on a Deck.
- Under Proton, picking a picture through **Change artwork...** / **Change banner...** (Wine's file dialog) is not
  verified yet. Pictures you already chose are kept across restarts.
- Steam Deck / Linux: the launcher runs under Proton / Wine; there is no native Linux build.
