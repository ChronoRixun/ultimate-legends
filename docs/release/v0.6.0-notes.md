# Ultimate Legends 0.6.0

## New in beta 1

### Your own sidebar icons
Open a game's card menu (right-click, the keyboard menu key, or **Y** on a controller) and choose **Change icon...**.
Pick a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture, check the square preview and choose **Use artwork**. The sidebar shows your icon from
then on, including after a restart. **Reset icon** puts back the original icon and keeps your card and banner art;
**Reset artwork** resets all of that game's custom pictures.

Like card and banner art, icons stay on your PC: nothing is uploaded or shared.

### Known issues in beta 1
- Picking a picture through the file dialog under Proton (Steam Deck / Linux) is not verified yet.
