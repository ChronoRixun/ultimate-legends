# Ultimate Legends 0.7.0

A test build for the Beta update channel. Stable players stay on 0.6.0.

## A new X-Men Legends page
The X-Men Legends (Legends Classic) page is split into tabs, so everything is easier to find:
- **Overview**, **Online**, **Display & options**, **Characters**, **Mods** and **Build & tools**.
- **Play** and a short status line stay at the top of every tab. When the game needs you (not set up yet, building,
  damaged, needs XML2 Fix, or a build that didn't finish), the status line says so and has the button you need:
  Set up, Build, Cancel, Resume, Repair or Install XML2 Fix.
- Tabs with nothing in them yet are hidden: before your first build you only see Overview and Build & tools.
- The page opens on Overview the first time, then on the last tab you picked. A build or a problem with the game
  opens Build & tools once.
- **Display & options** has the display settings, Remastered, the Vulkan renderer, Discord and the Classic
  conversation box. **Build & tools** has the build, which update channel the builder and XML2 Fix follow, and Test
  mode, folded away under "For testers".
- Keyboard: **Ctrl+PageUp / Ctrl+PageDown** change tab. Controller: **LT / RT** change tab (LB / RB still move
  through the sidebar).
- Every game page now has a smaller banner at the top, so more of the page fits on screen.

## Remastered settings
On the X-Men Legends and X-Men Legends II pages, **Display & options > Remastered** turns on XML2 Fix's optional
graphics settings: anti-aliasing, texture filtering, filtering of sharp-looking world art and centered Advanced
options menus on wide screens. They need **XML2 Fix 1.12.0-beta.1 or later** (a Beta channel build); with an older
Fix the section tells you to update. Off keeps the original look, and your choices are kept for when you turn it on
again. Changes apply the next time you start the game.

## Optional Vulkan renderer (DXVK), X-Men Legends only
**Display & options > Vulkan renderer (DXVK)** is off by default. When you turn it on, the launcher first tells you
what it will download (DXVK 3.1.1 from its official GitHub release, about 17 MB) and asks you to confirm. It checks
the download and puts only DXVK's two 32-bit files (d3d8.dll and d3d9.dll) next to the game. Turning it off removes
exactly those two files. If your game folder already has its own d3d8.dll or d3d9.dll, the launcher leaves it alone.
A Rebuild or Repair of the port may remove the files; the launcher puts them back from the saved download without
downloading again. On Steam Deck and Linux, Proton already uses its own DXVK, so the option stays off there. Your
graphics driver must support Vulkan. It is offered on X-Men Legends only for now.

## Known issues
**Support > Known issues** now lists what is still open in Legends Classic 0.6.1, including the online requirement:
everyone in a party needs **XML2 Fix 1.11.2 or 1.12.0-beta.2** (the internet play fix). A PC with an older XML2 Fix
is refused or its join times out.

## Known limitations of this beta
- The tabs, Remastered and DXVK were checked with the launcher's automated tests and in the real launcher window on
  Windows; DXVK was not yet downloaded from GitHub by the launcher in a test, and controller triggers (LT / RT) on
  a Steam Deck still need a check.
- A Repair can list DXVK's two files as extra files in Verify build until the next Legends Classic builder knows them.
